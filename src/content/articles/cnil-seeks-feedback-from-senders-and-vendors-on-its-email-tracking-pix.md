France's CNIL has opened a call for contributions on how organisations are applying its **recommendation on tracking pixels in emails**, which it published on 14 April 2026. That recommendation followed a public consultation that closed on 24 July 2025.

The recommendation took a phased approach. For new customers, senders were expected to comply with it in full. For pre-existing contact databases, senders only had to inform recipients clearly, within three months, that pixels were being used, so that recipients could easily object.

The call is aimed at private-sector companies, including technical providers and organisations that send email. The CNIL wants precise and, where possible, quantified feedback, particularly on economic sustainability. It is also asking about compliance costs, changes in audience measurement and personalisation, and alternative solutions developed by the market. Entities within the same group are asked to send a single combined response. **Contributions are open until 3 December 2026.**
