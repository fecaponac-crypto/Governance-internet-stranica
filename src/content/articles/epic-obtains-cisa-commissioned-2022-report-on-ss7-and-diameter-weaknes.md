The Electronic Privacy Information Center (EPIC) has published a redacted report on security weaknesses in US telecommunication signalling, obtained from the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) through a **Freedom of Information Act lawsuit**. AdaptiveMobile Security and Telecom Defense prepared the report for CISA in June 2022. It is marked as preliminary and states that it does not represent a coordinated DHS position.

The authors say their analysis of up to 50 billion telecom events a day shows the US is the most targeted country for signalling exploits across SS7, Diameter and GTP-C. They describe three incidents from March to May 2022, including **over 9,000 Global Opcode violations in seven days**.

According to EPIC, CISA withheld all 111 pages in November 2024. EPIC sued in June 2025, and CISA released the redacted text later.
