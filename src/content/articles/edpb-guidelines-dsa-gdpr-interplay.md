The European Data Protection Board has adopted **Guidelines 3/2025** on how the Digital Services Act and the GDPR interact where DSA obligations involve personal data.

Key points: notice-and-action processes should collect only necessary data and not require notifiers to identify themselves unless needed. The DSA ban on ads based on profiling with special category data applies *in addition to* the GDPR, even where a GDPR legal basis exists. Recommender options must be presented equally, without nudging users toward profiling, and no profiling data should be collected while the non-profiling option is active.

For minors, the EDPB advises against age-assurance methods that unambiguously identify users or permanently store age data based solely on Article 28 DSA. Very large platforms should align systemic risk assessments with GDPR principles and integrate them with DPIAs where appropriate.
