The US Department of Health and Human Services' Office for Civil Rights (OCR) published a final rule on 19 November 2026 amending the HIPAA Privacy Rule. It finalises much of a 2021 proposal. The rule takes effect on 19 January 2027, with **compliance required by 19 July 2027**.

Covered entities must now respond to access requests within **15 calendar days instead of 30**, with one extension of up to 30 days. The rule also defines "electronic health record" and "personal health application", limits identity-verification burdens, and requires covered entities to post fee schedules for copies on their websites. It narrows the right to direct transfers to third parties to electronic copies held in an electronic health record. It also confirms that disclosures for individual-level care coordination are permitted.

Health care providers no longer need written acknowledgement that a Notice of Privacy Practices was received. According to Hunton, existing business associate agreements may continue under a transition period until 18 January 2028 at the latest.
