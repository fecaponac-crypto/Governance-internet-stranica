European Commissioner Michael McGrath told the IAPP Europe Congress in Brussels that simplifying the EU's digital rulebook means cutting duplication and compliance costs, not "lowering standards or weakening protections", according to IAPP.

On the **Digital Omnibus**, the Council finalised its position on 22 October, but trilogues have not started. McGrath hoped Parliament would adopt its stance in the first quarter of next year. Kai Zenner, an adviser to MEP Axel Voss, expected talks to run past the often-cited February 2027 deadline. McGrath said the GDPR proposals on the definition of personal data, pseudonymisation and SME record-keeping drew on CJEU case law and EDPB opinions.

He described the **Digital Fairness Act** as a horizontal "safety net" covering dark patterns, ads to minors, sensitive-data ads and AI chatbots. It comes with a revised Consumer Protection Cooperation Regulation that lets the Commission enforce directly against large cross-border breaches. Child-protection overlaps with the KIDS Act will be handled under the KIDS Act.
