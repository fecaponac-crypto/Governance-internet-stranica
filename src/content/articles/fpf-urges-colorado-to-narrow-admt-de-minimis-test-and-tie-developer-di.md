The Future of Privacy Forum (FPF) filed comments on **26 October 2026** in the Colorado Department of Law's rulemaking for the Automated Decision-Making Technology Act (SB 26-189). The Act replaced the Colorado AI Act and takes effect on **1 January 2027**.

The interim draft lists six considerations for deciding whether an ADMT output was a 'de minimis' factor in a consequential decision. FPF wants to keep the four on human review, independent analysis, expertise and authority, which it says mirror California and existing Colorado Privacy Act rules. It would drop the factors on organisational policies and on the volume or complexity of personal data, treating policies as rebuttal evidence under proposed Rule 6.1(B).

FPF also asks that developers give deployers enough documentation to meet their obligations, including system name, version and the categories of outputs generated, plus timely notice of material updates. The Department has not yet finalised the rules.
