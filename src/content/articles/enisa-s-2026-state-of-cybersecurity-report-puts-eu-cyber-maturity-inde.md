ENISA has published the 2026 edition of its biennial **Report on the State of Cybersecurity in the Union**, required under Article 18 of the NIS2 Directive and prepared with the European Commission and the NIS Cooperation Group. It judges the threat level to the EU as substantial, while overall posture has improved.

The EU Cybersecurity Index averaged **71.53 points out of 100**. Supply chain management for essential and important entities scored 89.91. CSIRT maturity (54.76) is described as established but uneven, national certification ecosystems (55.56) as split, and primary and secondary education (55.44) as an area to improve. National coordinated vulnerability disclosure policies rose from 7 to 17 countries since 2024.

The report sets out five directions with policy recommendations, including smoother cross-border and cross-legislative implementation, stronger crisis cooperation, and developing sector maturity according to criticality with resilience strategies for AI-enabled threats. Regulatory compliance was reported as the main driver of cybersecurity investment across critical sectors' organisations (70%).
