TDWI has published a Blueprint Report by Fern Halper, its VP of Research, on governing documents, chat, images, audio and video used in AI. According to a summary from Ataccama, which sponsored the report, a survey of 179 organisations with active AI initiatives and a governance programme found **44% named governing unstructured data as their most frequent challenge**.

The summary reports that **51% of high-success organisations use metadata tagging for unstructured data, against 11% of low-success ones**. It gives 55% against 11% for bringing governance in at planning and design. The report sets out four components, from source and origin to consumption and use, plus cross-cutting disciplines.

TDWI's webinar page says governance must also cover derived information such as extracted entities, classifications, summaries and embeddings. Halper presented the findings in a webinar on 13 November 2026.

*Editor's note: The survey figures come from a summary by Ataccama, the report's sponsor, and the TDWI webinar page gives no figures; we have not seen the full report.*
