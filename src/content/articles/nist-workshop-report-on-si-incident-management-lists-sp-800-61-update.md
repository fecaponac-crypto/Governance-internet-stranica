NIST has published **Internal Report (IR) 8621**, summarising findings from a workshop held on 14 May 2026 with more than 400 participants from government, industry, academia and other communities. It examines the management of what NIST calls 'super intelligence (SI)' incidents, described by CDO Magazine as incidents involving AI systems. These cover both cyberattacks on such systems and harms from misuse, malfunction or unintended actions.

The report reviews current practice, existing guidance, preparedness gaps and the roles of the organisations involved. Areas for future work include **updating NIST's incident response guidance, including SP 800-61**, exploring new readiness resources for model developers, monitoring and evidence collection, incident information sharing, and clarifying coordination between developers, deployers and users.

According to CDO Magazine, the report suggests life cycles designed for conventional software may be incomplete for probabilistic, autonomous systems. It does not set a mandatory standard; NIST says it will inform further stakeholder engagement.
