Norway's data protection authority, Datatilsynet, said it had received personal data breach notifications linked to the Amadeus case, including from **Berg-Hansen Reisebureau AS and the Norwegian group**. The regulator said it was working to establish what happened and how extensive and serious the incident is.

Amadeus IT Group supplies systems used across Norwegian travel for booking flights, hotel rooms and rental cars. According to Norway's National Security Authority (NSM), a threat actor had access to Amadeus systems for **at least three years**, covering a range of personal data categories; the breach has reportedly been closed.

Director Line Coll said overall digital resilience depends on the security of each organisation and its supply chains. The authority warned that attackers may use booking details to send convincing follow-up scam messages, and advised individuals to change passwords and enable two-factor authentication.
