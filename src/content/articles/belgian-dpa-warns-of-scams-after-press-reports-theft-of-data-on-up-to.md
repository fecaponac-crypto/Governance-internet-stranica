Belgium's Autorité de protection des données (APD) published a warning on 25 November 2026 after the press reported that data on potentially more than a million residents of Flemish municipalities **may have been hacked**. The APD's headline refers to **1.2 million Flemish residents**.

According to the press reports the APD cites, the data may include names, addresses, national register numbers and IBAN bank account numbers. The authority warns that such data can be used for convincing scam messages by email, SMS and WhatsApp, and in phone calls. It adds that AI tools can quickly produce personalised texts that seem more credible, especially when attackers hold more information about a person.

The APD says it is following the case closely. It advises citizens to verify callers through official channels, avoid links in messages, enable two-factor authentication and delete unused accounts. It plans to publish a thematic dossier on data leaks on its website.
