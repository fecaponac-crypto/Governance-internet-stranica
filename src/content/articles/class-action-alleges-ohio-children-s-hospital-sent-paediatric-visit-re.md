A mother of two minor patients filed a putative class action on **20 November 2026** against Dayton Children's Hospital in the US District Court for the Southern District of Ohio (No. 3:26-cv-00422). She alleges the hospital used an AI documentation tool to record paediatric visits and transmit the conversations to the tool's vendor without meaningful notice or informed consent.

The complaint further alleges that the vendor **retained recordings or transcripts and used encounter data to improve its commercial AI models**. Claims are brought under federal and state wiretap laws, as well as for breach of confidence, intrusion upon seclusion and breach of implied contract.

The allegations have not been tested in court. According to Robinson+Cole, which reported the case, the central issue is whether patients and parents understood that the full conversation could be recorded, sent outside the hospital and used by a third party beyond their care.
