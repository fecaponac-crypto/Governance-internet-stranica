The League of United Latin American Citizens (LULAC) and the Electronic Privacy Information Center (EPIC) filed a motion on **29 October 2026** in the US District Court for the District of Columbia. It seeks a temporary restraining order, a stay or a preliminary injunction against the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) and the Department of Justice (DOJ).

The motion targets the agencies' "Voter Data Transmission Policy". Under this policy, data on registered voters the agencies claim are likely noncitizens is sent to states and localities. Election officials may then investigate those voters, segregate their ballots or withhold tabulation. The movants allege violations of the **Privacy Act** and the Administrative Procedure Act. According to EPIC, DOJ has already sent lists to California and Washington, and the lists have "repeatedly proven to be unreliable".

The plaintiffs ask the court to rule by 2 November. They also want the agencies to file a status report within 24 hours of any order being issued, confirming compliance and identifying all voter data already disclosed and its recipients.
