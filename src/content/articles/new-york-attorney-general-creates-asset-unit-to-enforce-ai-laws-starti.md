New York Attorney General Letitia James has announced the **AI Safety, Standards, Enforcement and Transparency (ASSET) Unit** within her office. CDO Magazine dated the announcement 17 November. The unit will lead enforcement of the Responsible AI Safety Education (RAISE) Act and other AI safety laws. It will also develop policy proposals and provide technical expertise to investigations across the office.

The RAISE Act **takes effect on 1 January 2027**. It requires large frontier AI model developers to disclose their safety measures publicly and to report security and safety incidents promptly. The unit's remit also covers data privacy, digital scams, cybersecurity and online safety. The office lists the SHIELD Act's data security duties and the recently signed youth chatbot law among the powers it can use.

The unit sits in the Economic Justice Division and works with the Bureau of Internet and Technology. Its team is still being recruited under Cristy Phillips.
