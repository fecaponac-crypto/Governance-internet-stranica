The US Federal Trade Commission has filed an amended complaint adding **Arizona, Florida, Nebraska, Ohio and Wisconsin** as co-plaintiffs in its lawsuit against telehealth provider Hims & Hers. Utah and California, through Los Angeles County Counsel, were already partners in the case, first brought in July.

The complaint alleges that Hims told consumers their health information would stay private while sharing it with Meta, Snap and other third parties. It also alleges the company charged for prescriptions almost immediately after an intake form was submitted, without clear disclosure, and made subscriptions difficult to cancel.

The claims now rest on the FTC Act, the Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act and consumer protection laws in seven states. The Commission voted **2-0** to authorise the filing in the US District Court for the Northern District of California. The allegations have not been ruled on; the court will decide the case.
