France's CNIL published guidance on 24 November 2026 to help transport and parcel delivery operators work out their roles under the GDPR. The reading grid builds on criteria set out by the European Data Protection Board in its guidelines of 7 July 2021.

According to the CNIL, **the carrier can generally be qualified as a controller**, because it alone decides the purposes (such as routing and tracking parcels) and essential means of processing — its own database, retention periods, which data to collect and how to contact recipients by SMS or email — and receives no instructions from the sender. Only in rare cases, where the carrier acts on the sender's instructions and tracking is run jointly with the sender, is it a processor.

The CNIL stresses that GDPR roles are separate from commercial subcontracting of delivery services, and that **organisations must document and be able to justify their qualification**, for example during a CNIL inspection.
