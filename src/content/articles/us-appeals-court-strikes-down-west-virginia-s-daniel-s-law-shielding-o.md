The US Court of Appeals for the 4th Circuit on 27 October 2026 upheld a district court ruling that **W. Va. Code § 5A-8-24(e) violates the First and Fourteenth Amendments**. The provision bars disclosure of the home addresses or unpublished phone numbers of judges, prosecutors, public defenders and police officers without written permission, with damages of at least $1,000 per violation.

A retired officer had brought five class actions against people-search firms including Thomson Reuters, Whitepages, Thryv, PeopleConnect and LexisNexis Risk Solutions. The court agreed the safety interest was compelling but held the law was not the least restrictive means: liability arose without the discloser knowing the data concerned a protected person and **without prior notice and a removal request**, which other states' versions require.

According to law firm Clark Hill, the ruling could influence ongoing Daniel's Law litigation in New Jersey.
