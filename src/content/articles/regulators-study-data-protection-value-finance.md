The French, UK and Luxembourg data protection authorities have published a joint study, *From data protection to value creation in finance*, covering data valuation, accounting, mergers and acquisitions, and governance.

The study argues that data protection builds trust with customers, partners, investors and authorities, differentiates organisations with robust data governance, and strengthens risk management. Weak governance or non-compliance, in turn, can reduce the economic value of a data asset.

It sets out practices for factoring data and compliance into financial **due diligence** and discounted cash flow valuations, and discusses recognising data as an intangible asset under **IFRS** — noting that current standards would merit evolving. The findings combine interviews with auditors, consultants, law firms and financial authorities, and a survey of several hundred companies in France and Luxembourg.
