Monte Carlo has launched **Bayes**, which it describes as an AI teammate that combines its platform capabilities to detect, troubleshoot and fix issues across AI agents and the data feeding them. The company says Bayes learns a customer's data and AI environment once connected and runs a loop based on reinforcement learning principles to keep detecting and correcting problems.

Customers can hand over a single task, such as troubleshooting an alert, or a standing mandate to set up monitoring, triage and execute fixes in the background. According to Monte Carlo, Bayes helps enterprises detect **200% more incidents** than in-house systems while flagging over 80% fewer false positives; these figures have not been independently verified.

Bayes is available to existing customers in the Monte Carlo platform, Slack, Microsoft Teams and via MCP. It also extends the company's partnership with Snowflake.
