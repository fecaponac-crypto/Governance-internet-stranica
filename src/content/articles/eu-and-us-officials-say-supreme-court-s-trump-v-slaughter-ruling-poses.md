US and EU officials told the IAPP Europe Congress 2026 that the US Supreme Court's ruling in Trump v. Slaughter does not undermine the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF). In that judgment, decided on 29 June 2026, the Court held that the FTC's for-cause removal protection for commissioners **violates the separation of powers**.

Alex Greenstein of the US Department of Commerce said he saw no practical impact, as the FTC remains obliged to enforce companies' DPF commitments. According to Commerce, the DPF had **more than 3,700 participating entities** as of November 2026. The Commission's Louisa Klingvall said the matter was under "ongoing assessment" with US officials, following a July EDPB request for a review. She said other GDPR transfer tools were unaffected.

A CJEU ruling on Philippe Latombe's appeal is still awaited, and Max Schrems has said a new challenge is being drafted.
