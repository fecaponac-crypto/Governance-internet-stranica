The Electronic Privacy Information Center (EPIC) submitted comments on **23 November 2026** opposing a modified System of Records Notice for the State Department's Passport Services Records system, published on 23 October. According to EPIC, the notice adds routine use (w), allowing records to be shared with government agencies and unspecified **non-profit organisations** to verify identity, citizenship, "voter eligibility" and "other government services".

EPIC argues that voter verification is not compatible with the system's original purposes of passport adjudication, benefits verification and certain law enforcement uses. It says passport and voting eligibility depend on different legal criteria, and that a missing passport record says nothing about whether someone may vote.

The group also says the notice lacks security detail and accuracy safeguards, such as limits on drawing adverse conclusions from non-matches. It adds that no cited statute gives the department a voter-verification role. EPIC asks the department to withdraw the notice.
