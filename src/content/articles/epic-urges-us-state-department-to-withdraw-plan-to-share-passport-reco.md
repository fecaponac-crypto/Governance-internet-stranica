The Electronic Privacy Information Center (EPIC) submitted comments on **23 November 2026** opposing a modified System of Records Notice for the State Department's Passport Services Records system, published on 23 October. The notice's new routine use (w) allows records to be shared with government agencies and **non-profit organisations** using federal verification platforms, to verify identity or citizenship for users of government websites, "voter eligibility" and "other governmental services". Routine uses (u), (v) and (w) were due to take effect on 23 November, the last day for comments, unless comments required a revision.

The notice describes the system as used to establish nationality and identity for purposes including voter eligibility. EPIC argues that voter verification is not compatible with the purposes for which the records were collected. It says passport and voting eligibility depend on different legal criteria, and that a missing passport record says nothing about whether someone may vote.

The group also says the notice lacks security detail and accuracy safeguards, such as limits on drawing adverse conclusions from non-matches. It adds that no cited statute gives the department a voter-verification role. EPIC asks the department to withdraw the notice.

*Editor's note: The objections come from EPIC, an advocacy group; the notice's terms are taken from the Federal Register.*
