California Governor Gavin Newsom's office released recommendations on 18 November to strengthen the state's AI safety framework. They were drawn up by the Government Operations Agency with an expert group and the Office of Emergency Services.

The report proposes that the state, rather than companies, should set **baseline standards for all AI developers**. Developers would undergo independent safety testing and verification at every stage of model training, development and deployment. They would also face expanded safety incident reporting and need verified risk-containment protocols. The state would gain **authority to order an emergency shutdown** and to cap recursive self-improvement, in which a model designs its successor without human input.

The advisers included Jason Goldman, Gillian Hadfield, Alondra Nelson and Rob Reich. The executive order that convened them also told agencies to fast-track SB 813 and AB 1405, on independent verification organisations and a registry of AI auditors. Newsom called on Congress and President Trump to adopt the framework or treat it as a floor.
