Italy's **Legislative Decree No. 179/2026 entered into force on 23 October**, according to an IAPP contributor article. Published in the Official Journal on 8 October, it follows Law No. 132 of September 2025, Italy's general AI law, which asked the government to align national law with the EU AI Act.

The decree defines the roles of the two authorities named in Law No. 132. The Agency for Digital Italy (AgID) is the notifying authority. It assesses, designates and audits conformity assessment bodies and can restrict, suspend or withdraw designations.

The **National Cybersecurity Agency (ACN) is the main market surveillance authority** and the single point of contact with EU institutions. It has investigative and enforcement powers, including unannounced inspections of real-world testing. It also handles national registration of high-risk systems used in critical infrastructure (Annex III, point 2). The article says Italy has made distinctive choices in at least two areas.
