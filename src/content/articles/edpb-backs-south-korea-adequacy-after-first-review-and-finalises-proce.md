On 10 November 2026 the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) adopted a statement on the first review of the EU adequacy decision for South Korea. It welcomed amendments to Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), including a general right to withdraw consent, a right to request suspension of processing, and a right to object to, or seek an explanation of, **automated decisions, including those made with AI**, that significantly affect individuals. The Board also endorsed the Commission's proposal to move to a four-year review cycle.

The EDPB also adopted, after public consultation, final recommendations on binding corporate rules for processors (BCR-P). Holders, new applicants and pending applicants should align their rules, depending on their situation, either during the application procedure or **in their 2028 annual update**.

The Board also chose automated decision-making, notably AI-assisted recruitment systems, as the theme of its sixth coordinated enforcement action, to be launched during 2027.
