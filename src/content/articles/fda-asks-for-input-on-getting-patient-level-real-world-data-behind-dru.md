The US Food and Drug Administration has opened a request for information on improving its access to patient-level data used in real-world evidence (RWE) submissions for drugs and biological products, according to a 12 November Federal Register notice reported by CDO Magazine. The agency wants ways to independently review and verify results when real-world data (RWD) underpin applications. **Comments are due 11 January 2027.**

According to the notice, sponsors often do not own the RWD they use; hospitals, commercial data providers or other third parties may hold it, leaving sponsors without authorisation to submit patient-level records. The FDA says sponsors should agree terms with data owners that allow it to access data and inspect source records.

The agency also cites conflicts between data-use agreements and federal record-retention and Freedom of Information Act duties, privacy constraints under **HIPAA and the GDPR**, and the size of imaging, longitudinal and multi-omics datasets.
