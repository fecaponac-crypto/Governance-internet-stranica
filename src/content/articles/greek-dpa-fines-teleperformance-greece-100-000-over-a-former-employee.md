The Hellenic Data Protection Authority (HDPA) has imposed two fines totalling **€100,000** on Teleperformance Greece after a complaint from a former employee whose access request under Article 15 of the GDPR was not properly handled. The decision (no. 19/2026) is dated 5 October 2026, according to the EDPB summary.

The authority found that the company did not provide a complete copy of the complainant's data and **erased the data before his employment ended, although his legal claims were pending**. It also failed to justify clearly its extension of the response deadline, did not tell him the retention period, and lacked technical and organisational measures and procedures to keep the data available and to handle data subject requests lawfully.

The fines were €60,000 for breaches of Articles 5(1)(a) and (f), 15 and 17(3)(e), and €40,000 for breaches of Articles 5(2), 12(3), 13 and 32.
