The California Privacy Protection Agency (CalPrivacy) announced on 24 November that its Executive Director, **Tom Kemp, is leaving to take up a position outside the agency in January 2027**. He was appointed in March 2025. The agency did not name a successor.

According to CalPrivacy, the Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform (DROP) launched under Kemp is now used by **more than 600,000 Californians**, with an average of over 200 data broker deletions per user. The agency also cites sponsoring the Opt Me Out Act and the Expanding Privacy Rights Act, and setting up an audits division whose first audit covered gig economy platforms.

CalPrivacy says it has implemented regulations on automated decisionmaking technology, risk assessments and cybersecurity audits, and has opened new rulemaking on notices and disclosures, reducing friction in exercising privacy rights, and opt-out preference signals.
