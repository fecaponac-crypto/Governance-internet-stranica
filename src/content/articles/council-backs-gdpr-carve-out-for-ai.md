EU Member States have agreed their position on a key part of the **Digital Omnibus**, the Commission's package of changes to EU digital law. The text includes a new provision — Article 88c in the Commission proposal, Article 88bis in the Council version — under which processing personal data “in the context of AI” would generally rest on legitimate interest, without consent.

The Council added references to the existing right to object (Article 21 GDPR) and to controller accountability (Article 24). Member States have also proposed narrowing the definition of personal data so that pseudonymised data would often fall outside the GDPR.

This is not final law. The Council must still negotiate the wording with the European Parliament, which has not adopted its position, and the Commission.
