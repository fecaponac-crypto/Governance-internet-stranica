The Council of the EU's Permanent Representatives Committee agreed a **mandate for negotiations with the European Parliament** on the Digital Omnibus at its meeting on 21 October 2026. The text amends the GDPR, the Data Act, the AI Act, the ePrivacy Directive and NIS2. It also repeals the Data Governance Act and the Open Data Directive and moves public-sector data reuse into the Data Act.

The Data Act changes include a new ground for refusing data access where trade secrets face a high risk in third countries, the narrowing of business-to-government sharing to 'public emergencies' and the deletion of Article 36 on smart contracts.

According to noyb, the Council's **Article 88bis** would presume a legitimate interest for processing personal data 'in the context of AI'. noyb also says it would narrow the definition of personal data for pseudonymised data. Trilogue negotiations will follow once Parliament adopts its position.
