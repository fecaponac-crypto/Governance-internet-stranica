Identity management supplier SailPoint has published its *Horizons of identity security* report. According to the company, **89% of EMEA enterprises have deployed AI agents, but 99% of them lack the identity security tools to govern them**. The findings were released alongside its Navigate conference in London and reported by Computer Weekly.

The survey covered 340 identity security, IT, cyber security and risk leaders worldwide, 27% of them in EMEA. SailPoint says 54% of EMEA organisations remain at the earliest, most manual stage of identity security. It also says only 22% have mature capabilities for detecting shadow AI, meaning unsanctioned agent deployments.

Chief executive Mark McClain said every agent needs "a clear owner, full visibility and strict boundaries". SailPoint said it has added agent classes to its platform to detect threats and orchestrate other agents.

*Editor's note: This article relies on a news report of a vendor's own research; the findings have not been independently verified.*
