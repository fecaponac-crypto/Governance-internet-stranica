Collibra has announced the **acquisition of Trail ML**, a Munich-based AI governance company founded in 2023. Trail's capabilities will be folded into Collibra AI Command Center. The announcement does not disclose financial terms.

According to Collibra, Trail's agents analyse an AI system, determine which requirements apply from frameworks such as the EU AI Act, ISO 42001 and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, and then read evidence including documents, spreadsheets, technical documentation and code to judge whether controls are in place and effective. The company says findings link back to their sources, are updated as evidence changes and keep human review where required.

Trail also brings **runtime controls** that, Collibra says, let organisations define which tools an agent may use and block actions when policy conditions are violated. Collibra positions this as combining its catalogue of data, owners and policies with continuous control assessment.
