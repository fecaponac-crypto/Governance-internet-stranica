The US Federal Trade Commission has issued special orders under Section 6(b) of the FTC Act to **nine companies: Delta, United, JetBlue, Lyft, Uber, DoorDash, Instacart, Target and Walmart**. The agency wants to know whether prices, and gig-worker pay, vary between individuals based on personal data or loyalty-programme participation.

The orders ask each firm to document the inputs, tools and procedures used to set or adjust prices and compensation. The FTC cites driver data such as phone usage and offer-acceptance history as possible inputs to pay. Chairman Andrew N. Ferguson called the methodology "opaque".

The FTC says its 2024 study looked only at consultancies, not price-setters. **Recipients have 45 days from receipt to respond**, and the FTC can seek court enforcement. The Commission voted 2-0. A proposed enforcement policy statement on undisclosed personalised pricing went out for comment in August.
