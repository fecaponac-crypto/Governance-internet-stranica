Microsoft announced an **AI Transparency Initiative** at its Ignite conference on 17 November 2026. It says it is working with **more than 40 organisations**, including Accenture, Adobe, KPMG, Mastercard and Volvo Cars, on open standards for AI transparency, security and governance that apply regardless of model or provider.

The initiative has three parts. Microsoft will expand its participation in NIST's SI Agent Standards Initiative, co-develop standards with partners, and build the results into Microsoft AI, Foundry and Copilot. Its framework of 'see, verify, control' covers recording an agent's instructions, authorisation, model, data access and actions. It also covers checking model-reported signals against independent records and keeping evidence portable when providers change.

According to CDO Magazine, the effort is at an early stage. No technical specification, common audit-log format or implementation timeline has been announced.
