The European Commission published its **2027 work programme** (COM(2026) 710) on 4 November 2026. It confirms a broader "digital transformation package" and a review of the digital decade targets. It also announces a blueprint for industrial AI in five sectors: health, transport, agri-food, advanced manufacturing, and defence and space. Further actions target the AI stack, from chips and data to compute and equity-based funding.

The Commission says its 12 Omnibus packages could save **EUR 22.3 billion a year** in recurring administrative costs once fully implemented. It will pursue a Pact against gold-plating, under which member states would disclose national additions to EU law, backed by a 'review and remove' mechanism. It will also further assess the impact of data retention rules at EU level, with follow-up planned for the following year.

According to IAPP, the package could include a "Digital Fitness Check" that builds on the Omnibus changes to the GDPR, ePrivacy Directive and Data Act now under negotiation.
