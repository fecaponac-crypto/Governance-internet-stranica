The privacy advocacy group noyb has analysed **more than 1,000 amendments** tabled by Members of the European Parliament to the GDPR and ePrivacy Directive under the Commission's proposed Digital Omnibus. It graded each MEP and published the results on gdpr-omnibus.eu, searchable by country and political group.

According to noyb, the European People's Party (EPP) tabled the most amendments reducing privacy protections, including narrowing the GDPR's scope and broad exceptions for high-risk AI. noyb says the ECR and Patriots also scored negatively, Renew and the 'Sovereigns' scored zero, and **only S&D, the Left and the Greens scored positively overall**.

noyb states that Member States have largely agreed to the Commission's changes, leaving Parliament's position open. Its method subtracts negative from positive amendments, ignores neutral ones and does not weight their significance.

*Editor's note: This article relies solely on an analysis published by the advocacy group noyb.*
