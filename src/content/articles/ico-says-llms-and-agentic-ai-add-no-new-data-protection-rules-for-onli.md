The Information Commissioner's Office published a report on **13 November 2026**, titled 'AdTech 3.0', on how large language models and agentic AI interact with PECR, the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 in online advertising. It covers ads beside chatbot responses, agent protocols such as AdCP and ARTF, walled gardens, retail media and connected TV.

The ICO's central finding is that these technologies **do not create new data protection requirements**, but may change how organisations apply them and demonstrate compliance. Accountability cannot be passed to an agentic system. According to Hunton's summary, the report treats uncertain inferences of sensitive traits used for targeting as special category data needing explicit consent.

The ICO says the findings will inform its forthcoming work on agentic AI and automated decision-making, including the AI and ADM code of practice.
