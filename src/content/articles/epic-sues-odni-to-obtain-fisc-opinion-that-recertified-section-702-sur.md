The Electronic Privacy Information Center (EPIC) filed a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit against the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) on **24 November 2026** in the US District Court for the District of Columbia (case 1:26-cv-04171).

EPIC seeks the opinion of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court (FISC) of **17 March 2026** that approved the annual recertification of surveillance under Section 702 of FISA. Senator Ron Wyden has said the court found "major compliance problems". Significant FISC opinions must be declassified and released within 180 days, a deadline EPIC says passed on **13 September 2026** without publication.

Section 702 itself lapsed on 12 June 2026, but certifications already approved stay valid until they expire, so the programme is authorised until 17 March 2027, according to the complaint.

EPIC filed its request on 7 October. ODNI granted expedited processing but has made no determination, and the suit asks the court to order release. EPIC notes that the administration is pressing Congress to reauthorise Section 702 in the current lame-duck session.
