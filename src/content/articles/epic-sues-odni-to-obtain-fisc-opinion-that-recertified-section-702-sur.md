The Electronic Privacy Information Center (EPIC) filed a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit against the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) on **24 November 2026** in the US District Court for the District of Columbia (case 1:26-cv-04171).

EPIC seeks the March 2026 opinion of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court (FISC) that recertified surveillance under Section 702 of FISA after the authorising statute lapsed. According to a press release from Senator Wyden cited by EPIC, the opinion found "major compliance problems". EPIC says the opinion should have been declassified and released by **13 September 2026**, but had not been published.

EPIC filed its request on 7 October and says expedited processing was granted, but ODNI has made no determination. The suit asks the court to compel release. EPIC notes Congress may seek to reauthorise Section 702 before the lame duck session ends.

*Editor's note: This article relies on material published by EPIC, an advocacy group that is also the plaintiff in the case.*
