The UK Competition and Markets Authority has proposed designating Microsoft with **strategic market status** in its business software ecosystem and opened a consultation. A final decision is due by the February 2027 statutory deadline; designation does not imply wrongdoing.

The CMA found Microsoft has a strong position in all five areas it examined — PC operating systems, productivity suites, security software, server operating systems and relational databases — reinforced by switching barriers and integration advantages. Its roadmap for possible interventions covers whether customers can freely choose **AI tools other than Copilot**, how Microsoft bundles security products, and whether licensing limits which cloud customers can deploy on.

On cloud, the CMA reports that Microsoft and AWS have **removed data transfer fees** for customers changing provider, cut fees for multi-cloud use, begun interconnecting data centres with rivals, and introduced a route to request interoperability. Next it will focus on identity and access management across clouds and portable architectures.
