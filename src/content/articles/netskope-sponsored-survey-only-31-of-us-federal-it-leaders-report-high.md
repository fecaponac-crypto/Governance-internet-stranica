A survey of 275 US federal civilian and defence IT and security leaders, conducted by Market Connections in May 2026 and sponsored by Netskope, found that **only 31% report high or complete visibility into public AI tools and shadow AI activity**. A further 33% described their visibility as limited or non-existent.

Visibility was lower still for API-based AI interactions, at 24%. **Just 13% said their agencies apply security policy consistently** across AI agents, applications, endpoints, cloud and SaaS environments. Netskope also says its Threat Labs saw a fourfold increase in enterprise Model Context Protocol (MCP) traffic over ten weeks.

Netskope published the findings on 26 November, linking them to a Department of Homeland Security order requiring civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek products, including certain third-party products built on its technology. The company argues that AI use case inventories required under OMB memorandum M-25-21 do not by themselves reveal every AI interaction or enforce controls over sensitive data.
