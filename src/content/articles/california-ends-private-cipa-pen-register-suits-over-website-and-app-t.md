California Governor Gavin Newsom signed **Senate Bill 690** on 30 September 2026. It amends the civil remedy provision of the California Invasion of Privacy Act (CIPA). Pen register claims under Section 638.51 that arise from websites, online applications or mobile apps may now be brought against private actors only by the Attorney General. Both chambers passed the final text unanimously.

The law takes effect on **1 January 2027**. It also covers pending claims in actions filed on or after 1 January 2025. CIPA carries statutory damages of $5,000 per violation, and plaintiffs had used the provision to target cookies, pixels and analytics tools. Plaintiffs are expected to challenge the retroactivity, according to Privacy World, although the bill includes a severability clause.

The California Court of Appeal has asked the parties in Variety Media, LLC v. Superior Court whether the law makes that case moot. A decision is expected by mid-January 2027.
