European Digital Rights (EDRi) and its members have written to the **European Parliament and the Council of the EU**, which are negotiating the proposed Digital Networks Act (DNA) and Mobile Satellite Services (MSS) Regulation. Both would regulate communications service providers, including satellite-based ones.

According to EDRi, the proposals would let the Commission impose **data retention and interception obligations** on satellite operators through EU authorisation procedures and define how they work through comitology, which the group describes as "shrouded in secrecy". EDRi argues this bypasses democratic processes and threatens privacy and data protection.

The group says the move is premature because the Commission is still assessing whether a new EU data retention framework is needed. It also cites CJEU case-law holding that general and indiscriminate retention of traffic and location data is incompatible with EU law as a preventive crime-fighting measure. EDRi asks legislators to resist the powers.

*Editor's note: This article relies solely on an open letter published by EDRi, an advocacy group.*
