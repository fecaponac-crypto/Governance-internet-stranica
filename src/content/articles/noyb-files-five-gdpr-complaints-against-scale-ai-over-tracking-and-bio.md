Privacy group noyb, working with Intérêt à Agir, DIPLab and Reversing.Works, has filed **five GDPR complaints against Scale AI** in France, Italy, the Netherlands and Austria. According to noyb, Scale AI's Outlier platform engages more than 900,000 'freelance' workers who annotate data and rank model outputs for AI developers.

The French complaint to the CNIL alleges breaches of GDPR Articles 5(1)(c), 12(3), 15, 25(1) and 28(1), as well as the French transposition of the ePrivacy cookie rule. It cites traffic captures showing trackers from Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Fullstory, Sprig and others, which noyb says were placed without consent and sent names, email addresses and task status to third parties. It also challenges **repeated biometric verification via processor Persona** and an access request sent on 2 February 2026 that went unanswered.

noyb asks the authorities to investigate, including automated decision-making, and suggests fines. No authority has ruled.

*Editor's note: This article relies on noyb's own announcement and its complaint, both from an advocacy group; the claims have not been tested by any authority.*
