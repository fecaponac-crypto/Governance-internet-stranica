Democrats won both the House and Senate in the 2026 US midterm elections, according to IAPP, though votes in some races were still being counted. **The party takes control of both chambers in 2027**, giving it committee subpoena power, hearing agendas and markup authority.

According to IAPP, likely priorities include AI accountability, federal privacy legislation, children's online safety and FTC oversight. Rep. Frank Pallone, in line to chair House Energy and Commerce, is reported to be expanding inquiries into personalised pricing. Commentators cited by IAPP expect momentum for AI rules on safety and automated decision-making modelled on California, Colorado, Illinois and New York.

Democrats also flipped governor, attorney general and state legislative seats, which practitioners quoted say could revive stalled state bills. Any federal law would still need presidential approval or a **veto-proof majority**.
