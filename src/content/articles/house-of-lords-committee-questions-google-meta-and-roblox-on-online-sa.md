The House of Lords Communications and Digital Committee took evidence on **17 November** on the implementation and impact of the UK Online Safety Act. The witnesses were Katie O'Donovan of Google UK, Rebecca Stimson of Meta and Tim Scott of Roblox. According to IAPP, the Act is expected to be **fully enforceable in 2027**.

Google described locking on safe search for users under 18, using age inference where a user's age is unknown. Meta said the Act's risk assessment duties had codified its product risk process. Peers asked Meta about an open Ofcom investigation into allegedly inadequate risk assessments before Instagram's 'Instants' feature launched in the UK. Roblox cited facial age estimation, which splits its platform into age-based experiences.

Members also discussed Ofcom's compliance report, which, IAPP reports, found no significant change in harmful content in the first nine months of the children's duties.
