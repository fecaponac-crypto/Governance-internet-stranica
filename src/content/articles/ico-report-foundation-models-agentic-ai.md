The UK's data protection regulator has published a report after scrutinising ten of the world's largest AI foundation model developers operating in the UK. Its core message: developers must explain what personal data they train on, where it comes from and how people can object, and must document a valid lawful basis — including a legitimate-interest assessment where they rely on that basis.

The report also states that a model may itself fall within UK data protection law when training data is embedded in a way that allows **singling out, linkability or extraction**, to be assessed case by case. Special category data in training sets still requires a separate Article 9 condition, and the regulator warns that the usual candidates are unlikely to cover all such data.

Alongside the report, it opened a six-week call for evidence on agentic AI, which will feed into a statutory code of practice on AI and automated decision-making. Responses are due by **20 November 2026**.
