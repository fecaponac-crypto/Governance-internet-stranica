Spain's AEPD has published a guide on secure processing environments (SPEs) from a data protection perspective, written jointly with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority. Experts from Eurostat, Spain's digital ministry, Italy's Garante and the UK ICO contributed. It is available in English and Spanish.

SPEs let users access and process sensitive data, such as health data, without receiving or downloading it. The AEPD notes that the European Health Data Space Regulation makes SPEs **mandatory for secondary use of health data**, such as research and policymaking. The guide stresses that the SPE provider must retain **real and effective control** over infrastructure and processing, backed by technical measures, oversight and audit capability, and recommends avoiding single-supplier dependence.

It offers a risk methodology combining the 10 LIINE4DU threat categories with the Five Safes framework, reviews privacy engineering techniques, includes a six-phase health research case study, and states that a DPIA is generally mandatory.
