Ireland's Data Protection Commission (DPC) has fined Match Group, the owner of Tinder, **€11 million** for failing to give users a full copy of their personal data when they asked for it, according to POLITICO Europe.

The DPC opened its inquiry in January 2020. It examined whether Tinder's "Download My Data" tool, which users can use to request their data, complied with the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

In its decision, announced on a Friday according to the report, the DPC found that the tool **did not provide users with all of their personal data**. Some categories of information were left out, and in some cases users received only a summary rather than the data itself, the report says. The coverage does not say whether Match Group will appeal or what corrective steps are required.
