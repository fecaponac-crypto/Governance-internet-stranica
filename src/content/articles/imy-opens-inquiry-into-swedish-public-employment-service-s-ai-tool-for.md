Sweden's data protection authority, IMY, has opened a supervisory review of an AI system used by **Arbetsförmedlingen**, the Swedish Public Employment Service. The system is a statistical assessment aid that caseworkers use when deciding whether a jobseeker should join the labour market programme "Rusta och matcha".

IMY says it will examine how the system works and how the agency informs jobseekers about the processing of their personal data. It will also assess the lawfulness and necessity of the processing, which personal data are actually needed, and **whether automated decision-making occurs** in the system.

The review follows tips sent to IMY and media reports. According to IMY, AI in the public sector is a priority area for its supervision and guidance in 2026. No timetable for a decision was given.
