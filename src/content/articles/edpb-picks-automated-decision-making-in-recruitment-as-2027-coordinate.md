The European Data Protection Board has chosen automated decision-making in the employment context, specifically **candidate screening in recruitment**, as the topic of its 2027 coordinated enforcement action, according to IAPP. IAPP describes it as the sixth action under the Coordinated Enforcement Framework (CEF). Reviews will cover GDPR Article 22, the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing, and the right of access.

Under the EDPB's framework, **participation by authorities is voluntary**. Each authority may run fact-finding exercises, open new formal investigations or follow up on existing ones. A shared questionnaire or audit guide is drawn up, authorities launch at the same time, and national reports are combined into an EDPB report with findings and recommendations.

According to IAPP, the board has not explicitly done anything related to automated decision-making since 2018; as the Article 29 Working Party, it published detailed guidance on applying Article 22 in May 2018.
