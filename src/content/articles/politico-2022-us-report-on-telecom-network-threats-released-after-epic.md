An unclassified 2022 US government report on threats to telecommunications networks has become public after a lawsuit brought by the **Electronic Privacy Information Center (EPIC)**, according to Politico coverage highlighted by EPIC.

As quoted by Politico, the report said that unlawfully acquired data, combined with malicious access to US networks, created "the largest threat vector" for those protecting US subscribers' communications and their security. Politico's headline says the US knew of "perilous" telecom vulnerabilities years before the Salt Typhoon hacks.

EPIC filed the public records request in **2024**, the same year Salt Typhoon was first discovered inside US networks, according to the coverage. The report became publicly available on a Thursday, Politico said. The sources do not name the agency that wrote the report or detail its recommendations.

*Editor's note: This article relies on an advocacy group's summary of Politico reporting; the report itself was not reviewed.*
