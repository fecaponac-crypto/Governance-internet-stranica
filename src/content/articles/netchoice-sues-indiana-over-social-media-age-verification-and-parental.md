NetChoice, whose members include Meta, TikTok and X, filed a complaint on **18 November 2026** in the US District Court for the Southern District of Indiana challenging the social media provisions of House Enrolled Act 1408.

According to NetChoice, the law would require covered platforms to establish the age and Indiana residency of every user before they open or keep an account. Users flagged as possibly under 16 would have 30 days to prove their age, by parental consent or with 'transactional data' such as mortgage, education or employment records. NetChoice says platforms must delete parental-consent information but not that transactional data, and that the state would switch off personalised recommendations and features it labels 'addictive' even with parental permission.

The law is scheduled to take effect on **1 January 2027**. The filing does not settle whether it will.
