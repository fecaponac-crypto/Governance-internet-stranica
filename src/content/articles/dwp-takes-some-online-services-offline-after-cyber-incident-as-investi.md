The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) has taken **some of its online services offline as a precaution** after identifying a cyber incident affecting its systems. According to Computer Weekly, websites for pension, universal credit and personal independence payment claimants were affected after the incident was detected the previous day.

DWP said it is working with the National Cyber Security Centre and the Government Cyber Coordination Centre to investigate and restore services. It said **regular benefit and pension payments are not affected**, and reminded customers that it never asks for personal information by text or email.

According to Computer Weekly, the prime minister's spokesperson described a "live and ongoing incident" with "currently no evidence of data loss". The outlet reported that DWP had not reported any breach to the ICO so far, and that the ICO said it was engaging with the department to understand what happened.
