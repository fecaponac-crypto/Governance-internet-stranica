Nigeria's President Bola Ahmed Tinubu signed the **National Identity Management Commission (NIMC) Act 2026** into law on **26 June 2026** at the State House in Abuja, according to the presidency. It replaces the 2007 act that established the commission.

In a piece for the IAPP, Musa Odole Solomon, project coordinator of the Nigeria Digital Identification for Development (ID4D) Project, says the law centralises national identity management and mandates the integration of identity databases. He argues it addresses weaknesses that built up since 2007: agencies running identity databases in silos, inconsistent integration, unclear enforcement powers and funding structures that limited scale.

According to the author, the review began in May 2022, and the signing meets the conditions for releasing a second tranche of ID4D financing earmarked for enrolling people for National Identification Numbers. The first tranche followed the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
