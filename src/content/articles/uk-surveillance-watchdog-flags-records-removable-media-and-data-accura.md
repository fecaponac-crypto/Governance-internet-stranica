The Investigatory Powers Commissioner's Office (IPCO) has published its annual report for 2025, ordered to be printed by the House of Commons on 29 October 2026. It records **366,976 warrants and authorisations** issued across public authorities during the year and an increase in errors by the UK intelligence community and the National Crime Agency (NCA), with **four errors meeting the serious error definition**.

The report says GCHQ still lacks a clear picture of the scale, location and recoverability of lost removable media that may hold IPA-related data. The NCA could not consistently show that covert source and surveillance records were reviewed, retained or disposed of as the law requires.

According to Computer Weekly, faulty data from one mobile operator produced 72 false positives and 88 false negatives in IP address identification, leading to two wrongful arrests. Sir Brian Leveson also questions whether older regimes such as RIPA remain fit for purpose.
