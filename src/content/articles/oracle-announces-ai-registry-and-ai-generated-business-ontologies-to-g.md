Oracle has announced new capabilities for its AI Data Platform at Oracle AI World in Las Vegas, aimed at giving AI agents shared business context and giving organisations tools to govern a growing estate of agents. Oracle says the features will be available **within the next 12 months**.

An AI Discovery agent generates business ontologies from database schemas, semantic models, application and API documentation and unstructured documents; domain experts then review, refine and approve them. Oracle says the platform will monitor agents' use of ontologies and recommend updates for approval.

The **Oracle AI Registry** is described as a central inventory of agents, tools, skills and MCP servers, recording ownership, versions, dependencies, permissions and lifecycle status. A planned AI Governance and Control capability would add testing, policy assessment and monitoring of agent behaviour, quality, usage and cost. Oracle's own disclaimer states that timing and functionality may change.
