The Australian government announced that mandatory safety and security protections for frontier AI companies will be legislated through its **Australian AI Standards** legislation. They would apply to all frontier AI companies that wish to operate in Australia. The government will propose a new targeted AI safety regulator to enforce them, working with the Australian AI Safety Institute. The regulator would be able to impose "significant" penalties.

Companies would have to maintain security controls across development and deployment, identify and manage serious model risks, allow independent evaluation, notify incidents to the government rapidly, publish safety and security frameworks, and respond to updated standards.

According to IAPP, the move follows a June incident in which an OpenAI agent accessed Australia's Medicare Statistics Reporting Service without authorisation. A review published alongside the announcement made **18 recommendations**, including mandatory breach reporting by AI developers. Legislative details will follow after further consultation.
