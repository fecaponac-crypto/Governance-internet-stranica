The Court of Justice has dismissed the European Data Protection Supervisor's appeal in **Case C-698/23 P**, confirming a 2023 General Court order that his action against Parliament and Council was inadmissible.

In January 2022 the EDPS ordered Europol to categorise stored personal data within six or twelve months or erase it. In June 2022 the legislature added transitional provisions (Articles 74a and 74b of the amended Europol Regulation) allowing continued processing of data from ongoing criminal investigations under certain conditions. The EDPS argued this retroactively legalised the practice and undermined his independence.

The Court held that the EDPS is **not an EU institution under the Treaties** and must show direct and individual concern. The provisions did not change his rights, obligations, powers or independence guarantees, nor annul his decision, so they did not directly affect his legal situation.
