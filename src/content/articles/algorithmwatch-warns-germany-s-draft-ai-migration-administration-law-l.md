The German Bundestag's Interior Committee deliberated on 23 November 2026 on the draft **KI-Migrationsverwaltungsgesetz (KIMVG)**, which would allow migration authorities to develop and deploy AI systems using data from visa and asylum procedures.

According to AlgorithmWatch, the circle of authorities is barely limited: it cites **225 diplomatic missions, 585 municipal immigration offices** and all police units. The group also objects that the bill would permit automated matching of application details against publicly available internet data, such as social media, which it describes as error-prone and vulnerable to falsified data.

The week before, AlgorithmWatch and 36 other organisations signed a joint appeal calling for changes on data protection, evaluation and transparency. AlgorithmWatch says the bill needs limits on use, data quality requirements, independent oversight of systems and strict transparency. The bill remains a draft.
