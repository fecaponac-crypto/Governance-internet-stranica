The US Government Accountability Office (GAO) found that the Pentagon's Responsible AI Division had filled **just three of its 10 authorised positions** as of February 2026, according to Military Times reporting on a GAO report published on 14 October. It also found **29 of 64 efforts** in the department's Responsible AI Strategy and Implementation Pathway incomplete as of April 2026.

The Responsible AI Working Council did not meet from late 2024 until August 2025 and still lacks a formal charter setting out its roles and meeting frequency. GAO warned it is "at risk of lapsing again". Officials cited restructuring as a reason vacancies were unlikely to be filled.

The department agreed with GAO's two recommendations: charter the council and address the division's staffing. GAO also urged Congress to require earlier recommendations on defining AI responsibilities and workforce management.
