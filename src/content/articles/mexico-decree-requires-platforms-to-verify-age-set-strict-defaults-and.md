Mexico's President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo issued the **Decree on safe digital environments for children and adolescents**, dated 14 October 2026. It applies nationwide to "digital platforms", defined broadly to include social networks, video games and app stores.

Platforms must use age-identification mechanisms that go beyond self-declaration, publish criteria for age classification and content removal, remove prohibited content immediately and offer parental supervision. New accounts must default to the strictest privacy and safety settings. Platforms must also run periodic risk assessments covering design and recommender systems, share statistical reports with authorities, keep a 24/7 contact point for law enforcement and preserve evidence from removed accounts or content for at least 90 working days.

The decree took effect on publication in the official gazette. Platforms must document and report their compliance actions **within 30 calendar days** of entry into force. According to IAPP, it defines no supervisory authority or sanctions.
