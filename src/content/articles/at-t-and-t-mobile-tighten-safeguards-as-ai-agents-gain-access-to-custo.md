AT&T and T-Mobile are increasing cybersecurity investment as they expand AI across network operations, according to CDO Magazine, which reports comments their technology chiefs gave Reuters on 23 October.

According to the report, AT&T uses encryption, multifactor authentication and **human oversight of data access**. It is also working to verify whether **third-party AI agents requesting customer-account access** act on behalf of legitimate customers. AT&T CTO Jeremy Legg warned that third-party software and AI agents create risks beyond a carrier's own network, including both data breaches and network disruptions. T-Mobile CTO John Saw said the carrier is investing more in threat monitoring and response and working with vendors to close security gaps.

Both carriers already use AI operationally: AT&T to analyse trouble tickets and sometimes apply fixes automatically, T-Mobile to adjust cell-site antennas when nearby sites fail. Legg said fully autonomous networks remain "years away".
