Organisations are adopting agentic AI faster than they are building the governance to manage it, according to Gartner research reported by CDO Magazine. A Q2 2026 Gartner survey of more than 2,000 CIOs and technology executives found that **71% expect to deploy agentic AI within 12 months**, while **only 21% have agentic AI governance capabilities** today.

The findings were presented at Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo 2026 in Barcelona. A separate Gartner survey of more than 1,000 functional leaders found 58% seeing some positive value from AI, though organisations struggle to scale successful projects.

Gartner proposes a 'Value-Risk Flywheel' and a central, virtual decision-making body it calls a 'Flywheel Governor' to own AI safety plans and coordinate accountability, technology and economic decisions across functions without imposing identical standards everywhere.
