The European Data Protection Board (EDPB) adopted the **final version of its guidelines on legitimate interest** as a legal basis under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR at its latest plenary, according to Sweden's data protection authority IMY. The guidelines set out the criteria controllers must meet before relying on legitimate interest. They also include a practical flowchart to help apply the test. IMY says it acted as a co-rapporteur.

At the same meeting, the board chose **automated decision-making in recruitment** as the subject of its next coordinated action. The action will cover candidate screening and selection tools, including AI-driven ones. It will focus on the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions and on the right of access. National authorities can opt in over the coming weeks, and the work begins in 2027.

The board also adopted a statement on the review of South Korea's adequacy decision. It finalised recommendations on binding corporate rules for processors as well.
