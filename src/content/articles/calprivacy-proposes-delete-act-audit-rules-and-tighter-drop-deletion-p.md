The California Privacy Protection Agency (CalPrivacy) began formal rulemaking on 13 November 2026 to implement the Delete Act requirement that data brokers undergo an **independent third-party audit every three years from 1 January 2028**. The proposals cover audit timing, scope, auditor qualifications and independence, required evidence and report contents.

According to Hunton's summary, audits could not rely mainly on management attestations, and brokers meeting the definition between 1 August 2026 and 31 December 2027 would have to complete first reports by 1 November 2028. Amendments to the DROP rules would require brokers to retrieve deletion lists every 30 days instead of 45, search for matches across all records and systems, screen new data against suppression lists and report each identifier's status within 30 days.

**Written comments are due by 5 p.m. Pacific Time on 6 January 2027**, when CalPrivacy will also hold a public hearing. The text may change.
