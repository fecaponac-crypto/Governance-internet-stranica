The CNIL said it had issued **ten simplified-procedure sanctions** in November 2026 against French local authorities and one public body, following checks under its 2025 annual inspection theme on local-authority cybersecurity.

The security failings under GDPR Article 32 included no multi-factor authentication for administrators connecting remotely, software suppliers with permanent or shared-account network access, incomplete or unmonitored logs, poor network segmentation, unsupported server operating systems and weak or plaintext passwords. Some authorities had suffered breaches the CNIL said could have been avoided. Several had also skipped required security accreditation under the RGS, and some had carried out no impact assessment (Article 35) for parent-facing school e-services or for camera systems on public roads.

Fines ranged from **EUR 3,000 to EUR 20,000**, the legal maximum for this procedure. The CNIL said further procedures are under way and that it devotes half of its 2026 inspections and enforcement actions to data security.
