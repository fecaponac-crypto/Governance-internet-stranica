The European Data Protection Board (EDPB) adopted final guidelines on processing personal data on the basis of legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR on **10 November**, according to the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. They replace a first version that was published for public consultation in October 2024.

The guidelines explain the three conditions an organisation must meet. They also cover the rights people retain, including access, erasure and rectification. Worked examples address fraud prevention, network security and direct marketing.

An annexed flowchart sets out the **three-step test**: whether the interest is legitimate, whether the processing is necessary, and whether data subjects' rights override it. The assessment must be completed before processing begins, documented and kept under review. Where the interest is overridden, controllers may consider further measures such as an unconditional opt-out. If the interest remains overridden and no other legal basis applies, the processing must not proceed.
