Kai Zenner, head of office and digital policy adviser to MEP Axel Voss, said it was 'rather unrealistic' for Parliament's negotiations on the Digital Omnibus to meet the **February 2027** deadline, suggesting Easter as more likely, according to IAPP coverage of a panel at its Europe Congress 2026.

The Commission presented the package on **19 November 2025**, proposing targeted GDPR amendments, a single entry point for incident reporting, consolidation of data rules through the Data Act and changes to AI Act timing. The Council agreed its position in October; Parliament has yet to adopt its own before the three institutions can negotiate.

Zenner said pushback had exceeded expectations and that Data Act and trade secret exemption questions remained unresolved. A Commission official highlighted clarifying what counts as personal data, while Google pointed to legitimate interests for AI training.
