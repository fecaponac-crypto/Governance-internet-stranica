Representative Matt Van Epps (R-TN) introduced the **AI INSIGHT Act** in the US House of Representatives on 18 November, according to the House Homeland Security Committee. The bill's full title is the Improving Nonhuman-Agent Security, Identification, Governance, Handling, and Traceability Act.

It would establish a framework for securing AI agents operating on federal civilian information systems, with the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) providing operational support, guidance and technical assistance. According to Van Epps, it would build on existing cybersecurity programmes and controls to help agencies **identify which agents run on their networks, limit access to what their tasks require, monitor activity, and suspend or revoke access** when necessary.

The bill was one of four introduced by Committee Republicans that week, alongside measures on CISA's AI workforce, a cyber and AI investigations board, and IT-sector risk management. The sources describe only its introduction.
