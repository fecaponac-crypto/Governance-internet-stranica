Spain's data protection authority, the AEPD, has opened actions after a Maldita.es investigation reported child sexual abuse material circulating on TikTok. It will ask Maldita.es for the links where unlawful processing is said to occur, **require TikTok to provide specific information on how it removed the reported content**, and, once that information is analysed, order the blocking of specific links and content that involve unlawful processing of minors' data. The AEPD describes this as a targeted, proportionate measure.

The AEPD notes that TikTok has designated Ireland as its main establishment in Europe, making the Irish DPC the supervisory authority responsible for the platform under the GDPR. Its own options are limited to exceptional cases such as the **urgency procedure under Article 66 GDPR**.

The AEPD will pass the information to the DPC, the EDPB and other authorities where appropriate, and to the European Commission for the Digital Services Act. It says criminal investigation falls to police, prosecutors and the courts.
