Sweden's data protection authority, IMY, has issued reprimands to four companies running people-search services after supervisions under the GDPR. Legal Newsdesk Sweden AB (Lexbase) and Fuplex AB (Krimfup) processed personal data about crimes without a legal basis and are **ordered to stop users seeing such data when searching by name, personal identity number or address**. Nusvar AB (MrKoll) and Upplysning Checknode AB (Upplysning) failed to honour individuals' right to erasure.

All four hold constitutional protection through voluntary publishing certificates, which under Swedish rules disapply GDPR protections. IMY found these national exemptions go further than EU law allows, that EU law prevails, and that the services do not serve a journalistic purpose. It therefore applied **Article 10 and Article 17 GDPR**.

IMY says it has received around 3,800 complaints against the companies, and cites new case law from Sweden's Supreme Court and the CJEU.
