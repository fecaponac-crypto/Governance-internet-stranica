New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed Senate Bill 9051B/Assembly Bill 10379C on 28 October, according to IAPP. The law bars developers from offering minors chatbots with unsafe features grouped into **12 broad categories**, including pretending to be human, encouraging self-harm and engaging in lewd conversations with children.

Providers must verify users through several age assurance methods, at least one of which must not depend on government-issued ID, and must delete all user age data once verification is complete. The attorney general's office will run a complaints portal. Penalties reach **USD 25,000 per violation**, plus injunctive relief such as orders to delete unlawfully obtained personal data.

The law applies from 1 January 2027. According to a Future of Privacy Forum analyst quoted by IAPP, its prohibitions are broadly worded and could cause legal uncertainty.
