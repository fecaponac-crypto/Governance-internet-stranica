France's CNIL has announced that it imposed **38 sanctions under its simplified procedure** between June and October 2026, with fines totalling **€302,800**. Of these, 23 began with complaints. This brings the number of simplified sanctions in 2026 to 61.

Around a dozen fines concerned cookies: incomplete banners and trackers, such as advertising cookies, set before consent or after refusal. Around a dozen more concerned security failings, including passwords that could be a single character, generic customer-database accounts shared by several people, and outdated functions such as SHA-1. Six organisations were fined over marketing using contest-entry forms designed to steer people into consenting, and six for failing to answer CNIL requests.

Under the procedure, used since 2022, the CNIL president or a member decides alone, fines are capped at €20,000 and the organisations are not named.
