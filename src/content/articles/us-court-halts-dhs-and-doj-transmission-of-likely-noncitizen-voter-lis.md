The US District Court for the District of Columbia has granted a **preliminary injunction and a stay under 5 U.S.C. § 705** blocking the Department of Homeland Security and the Department of Justice from sending lists of supposed noncitizen voters to state and local election officials. The motion was brought by LULAC and EPIC in a suit also filed by the League of Women Voters.

According to the plaintiffs, the agencies gathered voter rolls from at least 47 states, enriched them with commercially acquired data and matched them against federal databases. The opinion notes that a Los Angeles County list of **more than 20,000 records** included only 7,035 with county addresses, and that a Nevada review found all 15,903 flagged people were citizens.

The motion argues the transmissions exceed statutory authority under the Administrative Procedure Act, breach the Privacy Act's disclosure limits and lack a published system of records notice (SORN). The order is preliminary; the case continues.
