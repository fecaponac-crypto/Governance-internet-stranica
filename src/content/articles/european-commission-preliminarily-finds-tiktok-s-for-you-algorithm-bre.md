The European Commission issued **preliminary findings that TikTok violated the Digital Services Act** on Friday, according to The Record. It said the 'For You' feed draws users into "rabbit holes" of legal content about sadness, self-harm and body image. According to the Commission, TikTok had spotted some of these risks in its own risk assessments but did not analyse them properly or set adequate safeguards. It also said the platform's tools to diversify the feed, refresh it or mark content "not interested" are ineffective.

Investigators reviewed internal documents and data and analysed around 20,000 videos. The Commission wants recommendations to rest on what users explicitly say they want, rather than implicit signals such as viewing time.

The findings are not final. TikTok can examine the evidence and respond. If the breach is confirmed, it faces a fine of **up to 6% of annual global revenue**.
