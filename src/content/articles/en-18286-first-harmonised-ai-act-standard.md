The European Commission has published the reference to **EN 18286:2026** in the Official Journal, making it the first harmonised standard supporting the EU AI Act. Developed by CEN and CENELEC, it covers the quality management system that providers of high-risk AI systems must operate under Article 17.

Because the reference was published through Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2274, providers that implement the standard benefit from a **presumption of conformity** with those requirements.

EN 18286 builds the quality management system around the AI system lifecycle rather than the organisation. It is designed to be layered on top of existing standards such as ISO/IEC 42001 or ISO 9001, and explicitly allows providers in regulated sectors to integrate their management systems. Further harmonised standards are expected to follow.
