The Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (AP), has published its **enforcement policy**. It explains how the AP uses its enforcement instruments when organisations do not comply with privacy rules.

The policy covers the principles the AP applies during the enforcement phase, how the enforcement procedure unfolds, and how the AP works with other supervisory authorities. According to the AP, organisations had raised questions about its procedure and what to expect during it, and the policy is intended to give them more clarity.

The AP published a draft for internet consultation on 13 April 2026 and received **12 responses**. It says these made the final policy clearer and more accurate. A separate accountability document explains how the responses were handled.
