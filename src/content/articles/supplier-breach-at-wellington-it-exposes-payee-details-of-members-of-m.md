Hackers exploited a vulnerability at Wellington IT, a Belfast-based software supplier, to access payee details held in credit union mobile banking apps it operates. Ireland's Data Protection Commission told The Record it had received **more than 40 breach notifications** from credit unions that used the company as a data processor. The UK's ICO said it was starting to see reports from credit unions, but none from Wellington IT.

According to Irish League of Credit Unions chief executive David Malone, Wellington closed the flaw on 6 October, but its initial investigation did not identify a data protection breach; **credit unions were not notified until 16 November**.

Minister of State Robert Troy said members without the apps were unaffected and there was no evidence funds had been accessed. Wellington has engaged external experts to establish how many accounts and users were affected.
