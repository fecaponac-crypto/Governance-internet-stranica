AlgorithmWatch, Agora Digitale Transformation, ISD Germany and Wikimedia Deutschland have submitted a joint statement on the discussion draft of Germany's **Digitale-Medien-Staatsvertrag (DMStV)**. With it, the federal states' broadcasting commission plans to bring platforms and AI services under media law for the first time. The roughly 186-page draft of Part 2 covers AI information systems, artificially boosted reach, the findability of journalism, media concentration, advertising and supervision.

The groups argue that, instead of percentage figures for the 'accuracy' of AI answers, chatbots should give verifiable sources, flag uncertainty and face regular independent testing, especially before elections. They also criticise a quota requiring roughly every fifth post to come from a privileged pool, saying it changes what appears in feeds but not what they optimise for. In their view, users should have alternatives to feeds driven by personal data, and regulators should gain audit rights and researcher data access.

The comment period closed on **13 November 2026**.
