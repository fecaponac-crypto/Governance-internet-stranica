The European Commission is due to present a new **Digital Fairness Act** on Wednesday, according to POLITICO Europe. The report describes the law as aimed at practices websites have used over roughly 20 years to extract more money from consumers.

The practices named in the coverage include **dynamic pricing that shifts ticket prices with demand**, cumbersome processes for cancelling subscriptions, and the near-complete replacement of customer service with automated systems, such as bots that do not let users cancel. POLITICO frames the act as the EU's response to a wider trend of consumer authorities curbing exploitative online practices.

The coverage does not set out the proposal's specific obligations, scope or timetable.
