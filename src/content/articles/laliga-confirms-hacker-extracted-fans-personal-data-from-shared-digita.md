LaLiga said on 23 November that an attacker had illegitimately accessed one of its technology platforms for fan services **between 14 and 16 November**, extracting personal data of users registered with digital services run by LaLiga and some of its clubs. Its security teams contained the attack on 16 November, and the league said other systems and competitions were unaffected.

LaLiga has notified Spain's data protection authority, the AEPD, and filed a complaint with the National Police's central cybercrime unit. It is still analysing platform activity to establish the scope.

According to The Record, **at least five clubs** — CD Eldense, Real Oviedo, Real Zaragoza, Córdoba CF and CD Leganés — confirmed fan data was affected. Eldense said identification and contact details were taken but, on current information, not passwords or payment data. Clubs warned of impersonation and fraud by email, text and phone.
